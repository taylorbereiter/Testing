ELSINORE

Kronborg Castle, Helsingør — the home of Hamlet

Walk a full-scale replica of the great Renaissance fortress on the Øresund. The court of Denmark lives out its day around you — and performs the scenes of Shakespeare’s Hamlet, hour by hour, in their proper places: the Ghost on the gun platform at midnight, the Mousetrap in the Great Hall, the gravediggers at their work. Tap the gold ⓘ markers to learn the real castle’s history.
